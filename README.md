# Stronghold — Harris Secured Networks client portal for Android

The client portal and billing app of **Harris Secured Networks** (PAC/SAFE HSN Stronghold LLC): invoices and
payments, remote console, team and account security — the portal at www.securednetworks.us as an app.

## Install

1. On your Android phone, open **[Releases](https://github.com/TMinus36/stronghold-portal-app/releases)** and download
   `stronghold-portal.apk` (or go to https://www.securednetworks.us/app/ and tap *Download the app*).
2. Open the downloaded file; allow installing apps from your browser once when Android asks.
3. Open **Stronghold** and sign in with your portal account and two-factor code.

iPhone: open https://www.securednetworks.us/app/ in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*.

Every release lists the SHA-256 of the APK. Signing certificate SHA-256:
`D8:30:6F:11:5F:B8:80:2A:02:8E:94:E8:23:2D:1C:D3:19:74:40:AA:81:59:9E:AE:AD:F7:17:8B:21:8B:E1:CC`
